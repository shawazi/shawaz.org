Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers this website, shawaz.org, including its AI assistant, and the personal software tools published by Shawaz Imam that sign in with a Google account, including Life Ops.

This website

The site measures its own visits with first-party analytics that run on the site owner's own server. There are no cookies, no advertising and no third-party trackers. When you visit, the site records:

  • the pages you view, when, how long you stay, how far you scroll, which sections you see, and the links and buttons you click;
  • the page that referred you, your browser, device type, screen size and language;
  • an approximate location (country, region and city) and the name of the network your connection comes from (for example, a company's network), both looked up from your IP address. The IP address itself is not stored;
  • a random visitor ID kept in your browser's local storage, so repeat visits can be recognised. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, none of this is recorded.

Visit data is kept for 13 months and then deleted. It is used only by Shawaz Imam to understand who is interested in his work, and is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

The AI assistant

The "Talk to my AI assistant" button starts a voice call in your browser with an automated assistant. It is not a person, and it answers only from the information on this site. Your microphone is used only during the call.

To answer you, your speech is converted to text and processed by AI models. The call's transcript, its length and its outcome are kept for 13 months, linked to your visit. If you leave a message (for example your name, company, role and a number to call back), it is emailed to Shawaz Imam and kept in his email. You can ask for a transcript or message to be deleted by writing to the address below.

Personal tools that sign in with Google

These tools are built for, and used only by, their developer.

Data the tools access

With your permission, a tool may read and modify Gmail messages, drafts and settings, and read calendar events, only as needed for the feature you use (for example, creating an email draft or a reminder).

How data is used and stored

Google user data is used only to provide those features. Sign-in tokens are stored on the developer's own computer and are never shared. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to third parties.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are then useless and are deleted.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: shawaz@shawaz.org. For the tools that sign in with Google, you can also write to shawaz.imam@gmail.com.
