Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy covers the personal software tools published by Shawaz Imam that sign in with a Google account, including Life Ops. These tools are built for, and used only by, their developer.

Data the tools access

With your permission, a tool may read and modify Gmail messages, drafts and settings, and read calendar events, only as needed for the feature you use (for example, creating an email draft or a reminder).

How data is used and stored

Google user data is used only to provide those features. Sign-in tokens are stored on the developer's own computer and are never shared. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to third parties.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are then useless and are deleted.
